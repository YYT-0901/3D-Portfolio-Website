### Overview

A performer inhabits a sparse room where memory is arranged like a set of physical cues. The film distills rehearsal, absence and presence into one repetitive choreography that slowly becomes a ritual of re-entry.

### Visual language

The palette stays restrained and tactile, letting gesture and silence carry the weight of the story.
