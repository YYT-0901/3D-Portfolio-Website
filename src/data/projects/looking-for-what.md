### Overview

Facing the belongings left behind by a former lover, he reconstructs a year of searching for love through a calm yet absurd monologue. After four chaotic romances, the fifth fades into silence, leaving one question unresolved: what is it that we keep searching for?

### Creative direction

The film moves between comedy and melancholy, balancing restrained framing with a deliberately theatrical rhythm. The goal was to keep the emotional truth intimate while making each memory feel like a slightly distorted scene from a life in motion.
